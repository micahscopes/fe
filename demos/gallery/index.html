Fe geometric-algebra gallery

Four draggable geometric-algebra sketches, each authored once in Fe. The picture is a @fragment shader the Fe compiler derived from the algebra (a CTFE-planned sparse contraction, not a hand-written formula); the controls are a Fe update_* fn your browser runs. Grab the geometry with your mouse.

What ganja.js interprets per-frame on the CPU, Fe computes and pins at compile time: the survivor mask is folded from the metric, the shader is machine-derived, and the same authored source runs native, in wasm, and on the GPU. JavaScript only marshals bytes; no pencil, quadric, or rotor math lives outside Fe. Where a browser exposes no WebGPU (the honest amber state), the Fe fragment still computes every pixel in V8.
